About

Positioning British Columbia’s creative sector at home and around the world

Creative BC

When Creative BC merged with BC Film and Media and the BC Film Commission in 2013, it presented a unique set of merging missions and initiatives that contribute to British Columbia’s overall cultural fabric in the province. This merger established a common ground between supporting domestic creators, and boosting the economy while emphasizing the business of creativity.

Now as the province’s designated economic development agency for the creative sector, Creative BC grew in size and scale, presenting an opportunity to define how its offerings work together, establish a strong sense of integration, and put itself on the map. We explored how we could better support these industries to innovate and thrive, both locally and globally. It was our first opportunity to create a cohesive brand ecosystem that truly reflects the sector as a whole—and the possibilities it represents.

Challenge

Today, Creative BC houses three primary service areas: the BC Film Commission, grants and programs for independent creators, and the administration of motion picture tax credits—alongside additional initiatives focused on environmental sustainability and workforce development.

The scale and complexity of this newly unified organization created challenges in communicating its wide range of programs, services, events, reports, and resources. In response, we initiated a visual identity refresh to modernize the brand and establish flexible systems capable of supporting a growing house of brands and sub-brands.

The refreshed identity retains the legacy “C” brandmark from the original logo, as well as the iconic pink, while introducing a Swiss-inspired design approach to bring clarity, structure, and timelessness to external communications. The colour palette draws inspiration from the dynamic palettes of British Columbia, reinforcing a strong sense of place. Preserving the integrity and history of Creative BC’s original brand remained a central priority throughout the process.

Result

Through my brand-building efforts, Creative BC and its initiatives achieved a significant global presence.

We elevated awareness in leading international markets, positioning the organization as a premier funding agency, film commission, and tax credit administrator.

Our work has been recognized as a benchmark for competitive jurisdictions worldwide, with notable success across web, social media, and brand touchpoints.
