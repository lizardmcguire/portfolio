Marketing LA’s leading ad photographers

The JUDE Group (Acquired by STADIUM Creative Group)

The JUDE Group, now a division of STADIUM Creative Group, is a Los Angeles-based photography agency representing talent that spans ad, documentary, motion, and entertainment photography.

As an independent consultant and digital marketing specialist, I led and managed social media strategy, supported with communications material, graphic design, and produced bi-weekly B2B email marketing collateral for photographers working with leading ad agencies and brands including The New York Times, Warner Music, NBC, Marshall, Nike, Fox, Gatorade, Time, and more.

Role

Execution and Result

Strategized and executed high-impact B2B email marketing campaigns on a bi-weekly cadence, positioning elite entertainment and journalism photographers to secure lucrative partnerships with top-tier advertising agencies and Fortune 500 consumer brands.

Delivered strategic brand consultation and custom graphic design services that elevated agency positioning and strengthened market differentiation within the competitive photography representation landscape.

Built and scaled Instagram presence from the ground up to 4K+ engaged followers through strategic content programming, community management, and data-driven audience growth tactics.
