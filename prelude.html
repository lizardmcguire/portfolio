About

Developing an impact fellowship program that redefines access to the film and TV industry

Prelude for Tender Pictures

Prelude was co-founded by Abbi Jacobson and Mieka Tennant with a clear vision: to level the playing field for entry into the film and TV industry. Through years of experience, Abbi and Mieka saw firsthand the unwritten rules of success, how access to networks, events, and insider knowledge often dictates opportunity. They understood what it takes to thrive, and more importantly, who often gets left behind, and how difficult it can be to gain the confidence to create.

Rooted in play, experimentation, and the belief that creativity flourishes before credentials, Prelude lives up to its name: a beginning, a spark, a moment of preparation for what’s next.

Challenge

The film and TV industry presents a range of challenges, from securing project funding to building networks and economic viability. Many careers in this industry are often inaccessible to those without the necessary funds, resources, and connections, making it essential to address these disparities with a priority on underrepresented communities.

Prelude was created to respond to these systemic barriers by creating moments of access and opportunities that directly challenge the industry’s exclusivity. Through targeted programming and strategic partnerships, Prelude opens doors for emerging storytellers.

Execution

I created foundational program materials including mentorship frameworks, curriculum, fundraising decks, and storytelling assets to support funding development.

I now support program operations and have established efficacy metrics and reporting systems to track fellow outcomes and program impact. Program partners include A24, Final Draft, The Criterion Collection, Motion Picture Association, and Masterclass.

I was brought on during the research and development phase and currently serve as Brand and Program Advisor. I supported end-to-end development of the 8-month fellowship for emerging filmmakers, conducting competitive landscape analysis and designing the application process. The curriculum was built upon foundations that are unique to other programs with exploratory play, confidence-building exercises, and exposure to filmmakers who started with similar barriers to the target group.

Result

In its inaugural year, the Prelude program supported ten emerging storytellers through a carefully crafted fellowship centered on both creative exploration and professional development. Each fellow was matched with a dedicated mentor, providing one-on-one guidance and industry insight through monthly mentor sessions throughout the program. Over the course of the eight months, fellows engaged with more than 30 established filmmakers and industry professionals through eight mentorship sessions and a dynamic slate of workshops, talks, and panels.

These sessions featured acclaimed writers, breakout independent filmmakers, and senior studio executives who shared valuable perspectives on both the art and business of storytelling. These experiences provided fellows with meaningful access to the inner workings of the film and television industry, from the creative process to career-building strategies.

Along the way, fellows developed foundational skills in shaping their point of view, refining their creative voice, and building confidence as emerging storytellers. Significantly, the program centered the power of the cohort—their ability to collaborate, uplift one another, and grow together as storytellers. Fellows developed meaningful bonds, finding creative partners and a sense of community that will continue to support their growth beyond the fellowship.
