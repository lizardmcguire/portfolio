About

I’m a Brand and Communications Strategist with over a decade of experience building impactful brands in entertainment, lifestyle, non-profit, and economic development.

Currently, I lead marketing at Creative BC, where I market British Columbia's creative industries to the world. I recently developed a film and TV fellowship for Tender Pictures, to redefine what access looks like in the industry.

Previously, I supported a startup, agencies, and small companies in building solid brand foundations that positioned them for growth.
