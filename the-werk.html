The Werk by Werklab

The Werk was the editorial voice of Werklab, a holistic co-working space reimagining the relationship between work and well-being. As Editorial Lead, I built a content platform from the ground up that challenged conventional work paradigms through journalism, thought leadership, and cultural commentary.

I established partnerships with freelance journalists from The New York Times, BBC, The Guardian, Time, Refinery29, and Teen Vogue, while curating perspectives from wellness innovators and progressive business leaders. The result: a compelling dialogue around purpose-driven work that resonated with a generation seeking more meaning from their professional lives.

Role

  • Editorial and Content Strategy
  • Creative Direction and Production
  • Social Media Strategy and Management

Challenge

Launched in the months before the pandemic, The Werk was asking critical questions about our relationship with work before these conversations became mainstream. We were exploring burnout, purpose, and the toll of hustle culture when most workplace content still glorified the grind. What felt prescient then became urgent as the world shifted, positioning The Werk at the forefront of a cultural reckoning with how much of ourselves we bring to work.

Strategy

I established partnerships with freelance journalists from The New York Times, BBC, The Guardian, Time, Refinery29, and Teen Vogue, while curating perspectives from wellness innovators and progressive business leaders.

I developed the editorial vision, voice, and content strategy aligned with brand positioning. I built the editorial calendar, content pipelines, and publication workflows, and established contributor fee structures and negotiation frameworks that allowed us to work with top-tier talent.

Execution

For content and production, I sourced, vetted, and managed relationships with more than 30 global freelance journalists. I directed creative production across over 200 visual assets spanning social media, web, print, and media kits. Working closely with creative agency Kastor & Pollux, I led graphic design and art direction while managing CMS implementation and optimization on Shopify.

I also spearheaded marketing and growth initiatives, leading social media strategy and execution across platforms. I wrote all brand communications, editorial copy, and promotional materials, and coordinated with external vendors and subject matter experts to ensure content quality and authenticity.

Result

Within four months of launch, The Werk established itself as a distinctive voice in the future-of-work conversation.

From a content production standpoint, I published more than 120 articles, including 30 pre-launch pieces that set the editorial tone. I built a contributor network of over 30 journalists across five continents and maintained a consistent weekly publishing cadence with high editorial standards.

On the creative front, I directed production of over 200 custom assets and illustrations, created a cohesive visual identity across digital and print touchpoints, and developed branded templates and design systems for scalability.

The audience response validated our approach. We grew our Instagram following by more than 4,000 in the launch month alone, established an engaged community around purpose-driven work content, and built a foundation for sustained editorial growth and brand authority.

This wasn’t just content marketing, it was editorial journalism with a point of view. By investing in real writers, original reporting, and substantive perspectives, The Werk carved out a unique position in an oversaturated market of productivity content and workspace advertising. We were asking the hard questions about work-life balance and professional identity before the pandemic made these conversations inevitable, giving the platform a depth and authenticity that pure trend-chasing could never achieve.
