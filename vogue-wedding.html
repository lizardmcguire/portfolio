Doing as little as possible for wedding stationery

Featured in Vogue

For this project, I was tasked with creating unique, eclectic designs for wedding stationery for a special ceremony of love. With direction from the brides, I created a subtle array of barely-there stationery complementing their impeccable curated taste. The wedding story rose to prominence, capturing the spotlight not only within the pages of Vogue but also on Pinterest wedding boards.

The wedding earned a spot in Vogue’s trend forecasting article that unveiled the most anticipated emerging wedding trends for the decade.
